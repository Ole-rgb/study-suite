---
description: Run a short, source-grounded quiz.
argument-hint: [course] [session]
---

Read the relevant `Course.md`, session note, and linked slides. Ask one question at a
time and wait for the learner's answer. Default to about eight questions and stop at
the cap. Do not quiz labs. At the end, summarize strengths and gaps, then ask before
writing progress or confidence updates unless the learner has already authorized that
routine logging behavior.
